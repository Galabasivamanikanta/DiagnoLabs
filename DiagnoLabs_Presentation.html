The Challenge
The Diagnostic Healthcare Crisis
🏥
80% Labs Unaccredited
Over 80% of diagnostic labs in India lack NABL/ISO 15189 accreditation, risking misdiagnosis from uncalibrated equipment.
💰
Opaque Pricing
No standardized pricing registries. Unverified operators charge arbitrary fees for standard diagnostic test panels.
🚑
Mobility Barriers
Elderly & immunocompromised patients face extreme difficulty traveling to distant urban accredited centers.
😰
Report Anxiety
Patients cannot interpret complex pathological parameters (TSH, HbA1c, Creatinine) before formal consultations.
Research Finding  Tele-consultations without verified lab data retain <35% diagnostic efficacy — Kumar et al. (2024)

Core Algorithm
Geospatial NABL Discovery Engine
Si = dhaversine(p, li) / [ 1 + (ωnabl · αi) + (ωrating · ρi / 5.0) ]
❌ LAB A (UNACCREDITED)
Distance: 3.0 km • α=0 • Rating: 4.0
S = 2500 m
✅ LAB B (NABL ACCREDITED)
Distance: 4.0 km • α=1 • Rating: 4.8
S = 2298 m
Result  Accredited Lab B ranks HIGHER despite being 1km farther — prioritizing patient safety over raw proximity!
